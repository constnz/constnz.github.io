Teaching and service

Since 2011 I have taught 16 courses at Universidad de Concepción and Vrije Universiteit Brussel, for undergraduate journalism students, master’s students in digital communication and the international MSc in Journalism and Media in Europe. I have supervised 15 BA, MA and MSc theses.

I review for Journalism, Journalism Practice and Journalism & Mass Communication Quarterly, and for the ICA annual conference. I have served on the scientific committee of the INCOM Chile conference, evaluated master’s programmes in communication for Chile’s National Accreditation Commission, and sat on the board of Medios UdeC. During my PhD I represented doctoral candidates on the council of the Dutch Research School for Media Studies (RMeS).
