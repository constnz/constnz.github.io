Consulting. Audience research for newsrooms, foundations and public institutions, including the people they are not reaching.

Services

Audience research

In-depth interviews and focus groups to understand how people use, trust, avoid or value your journalism, and what they need from it.

Trace-based interviews

Interviews built around people’s own phone and browsing data, to see how news actually fits into their digital lives rather than how they remember it.

Information needs assessments

Studies of what local communities need to know and where they go for it, for newsrooms, foundations and public bodies planning local coverage.

Workshops and training

Sessions for journalists and newsroom teams on audience engagement, trust and co-creation, designed around their own challenges.

Strategy and evaluation

Advice on engagement, loyalty and audience strategies, and evaluation of projects aimed at reaching underserved or disengaged audiences.

Research design

Support for organisations commissioning audience studies: questions, methods, sampling and how to turn findings into decisions.
